# UX220-Lab-1
My favorite item

Hello, my name is Peter, I really technology stuffs. One of my recent pick up is Fitbit Air, a collabration between Fitbit and Google. It helps me understand my health better with heart monitor, sleep tracking, exercise tracking and other features.

I have been using it everyday and everywhere, it is really light and the design is gorgeous. Morning, when I wake up it is already on my wrist, it tells me how my sleep was in Google Health app, the information is very transparency and useful. Lunch, I go for a walk to get some food and it tracks my walk, I can also enter the calories, fat, protein as options. Afternoon, when I hit the gym, it makes my heart beat during that session visible, I can see if i am doing good and not too much. After the exercises, it tell me how much calories did I burn and how is my heart beat during that time and after that (rest). Sometimes I do not even feel like I am wearing something on my wrist. Big tech in such a small device.
